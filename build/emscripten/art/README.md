`pit-and-pom.png` is a game-ready illustration generated from the user's visual
reference, preserving the animals' markings, poses and garden composition.
Pituca/Pit is the black-and-white dog; Pompom/Pom is the orange-and-white cat.
The PNG is served separately from WebAssembly. Prompt and provenance are in
`docs/garden-assets.json`.
